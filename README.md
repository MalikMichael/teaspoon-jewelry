# Teaspoon Jewelry

A responsive, luxury-inspired starter storefront for Teaspoon Jewelry.

## Included
- Responsive homepage and product collection
- Working shopping bag/cart
- WhatsApp order handoff (add your WhatsApp number in `script.js`)
- Custom-order call to action
- No paid database or hosting dependency

## Before launch
1. Replace sample product names, prices and photos with the real collection.
2. In `script.js`, set `WHATSAPP_NUMBER` to your WhatsApp number in international digits-only format. For Ghana, start with `233` and omit the leading zero.
3. Update the email and Instagram handle in `index.html`.
4. Review all product prices and business details.

This is a static starter site. No online card or Mobile Money payment is processed yet; customers confirm orders and payment with you on WhatsApp.
